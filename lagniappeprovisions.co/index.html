Our story

Made the way things used to be made

Lagniappe Provisions started with a simple observation: the best things in a Louisiana kitchen rarely come from a supermarket shelf. They come from somebody who took their time — and then threw in a little extra when they handed it over.

We work in small batches, source as close to home as we can, and put our name on every label. No shortcuts, no filler, no fine print. Just provisions we’d be proud to set on our own table.

  • Small batch. Made in runs we can stand behind, not warehouses we’ve never seen.
  • Sourced close to home. Gulf South first, Louisiana whenever we can.
  • Always lagniappe. Every order ships with a little something extra. That’s the whole point.
